Binary Matching Income

For every matched RP pair (1 RP from Left leg + 1 RP from Right leg), you earn ₹600. Unmatched BV carries forward to the next calculation.

1 RP Left + 1 RP Right = ₹600 | Weaker leg determines matching | Carry forward prevents loss

Achievement Rank Rewards & Total Earnings

When the BV of your weaker leg reaches milestone thresholds, you unlock Achievement Ranks. When a member enters the 10th achievement (Chairman), they earn a total of ₹1.20 Crore in Binary Commission + ₹36 Lakh in Milestone Rewards = ₹1.56 Crore Total Income!

Total Earnings = Binary Matching Commission (₹600/RP pair) + One-Time Milestone Reward
